## About the fact in the film

The twist is based on Khait et al., "Sounds emitted by plants under stress are airborne and informative", Cell, 2023: drought-stressed tomato and tobacco plants emitted ultrasonic pops that microphones could record. If someone asks in the comments, that is the source.
